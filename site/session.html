Step by step

The shape of a session.

  1. Arrive and orient

    A short explanation of how it works. Then ask me anything.

  2. Tell one story

    Any problem, big or small. One concrete story, no spiritual language. Three minutes on a timer.

  3. I read the code

    I listen for the pattern underneath your words.

  4. Drop in

    We breathe. You let go of the concrete world and rest as the observer.

  5. Feel the pattern

    I name it, and you feel it as energy, like a stone dropped in still water. When the mind takes over, I guide you deeper.

  6. Dissolve, then install

    The tangle dissolves, and the antidote code is anchored in its place.

  7. Come back

    Slowly. Gently. Your recording is yours to keep.
