The tangle

Cut the root. Stop pruning branches.

Say eight of those patterns are tangled together down in the code.

Whatever you're facing right now is just the most recent manifestation of that deeper tangle. Work at the surface (the mind, the emotions, the concrete world) and it gets frustrating. Prune one branch, another grows.

Dissolve the tangle in the code itself, and it's like cutting out the sick root that's been poisoning the whole tree.
