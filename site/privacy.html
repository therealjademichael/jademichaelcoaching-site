Privacy

Your privacy, held with care.

What I collect, why, and who sees it.

Last updated November 24, 2026.

This is the privacy policy for jademichael.co, run by Jade Michael Coaching LLC, a Utah company ("Jade," "I," "we"). It covers this website and working with me. Plain words, no fine print.

The short version

  • I collect what you choose to give me, so I can talk with you and work with you.
  • I never sell your information. Not to anyone.
  • What you share in a session stays between us.
  • You can ask to see, fix or delete what I hold about you, any time.

What I collect, and where it goes

When you apply

The form on the Apply page is hosted by monday.com. What you type there (your name, phone, email, and what you're ready to shift) is stored in my monday.com account and read by me.

When you book

Connection calls and sessions are booked through TidyCal. It collects your name, your email and the time you picked, and sends us both a confirmation.

When we work together

  • Sessions happen on Zoom and are recorded. Your recording is yours to keep.
  • Agreements are signed through PandaDoc.
  • Payments are invoiced and processed through QuickBooks (Intuit). Your card or bank details go to them. I never see or store your full card number.
  • Email runs on Google Workspace.

When you just visit

This site has no ads, no ad trackers and no cookies of its own. Like every website, the company that hosts it (Netlify) keeps standard technical logs, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and safe. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the music player on the Artistry page comes from Spotify. Those services see that your browser asked for them.

Why I collect it

  • To answer you, book you and show up prepared.
  • To deliver your sessions and your recordings.
  • To invoice you and keep the books the law asks me to keep.
  • To send you news, only if you asked for it. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Who else sees it

Only the people and tools that help me run this business, and only what they need:

  • The services named above: monday.com, TidyCal, Zoom, PandaDoc, QuickBooks, Google Workspace and Netlify. Each has its own privacy policy.
  • My operations and bookkeeping support, who see business records like names, emails, invoices and payments. They do not sit in on sessions.
  • Co-producers. When I offer something together with another teacher or host, like a retreat, a course or a collaboration, the details you give to sign up are shared with that co-producer so we can run it together. The sign-up page will name them.
  • The law, if a court or a legal duty requires it, or if I believe in good faith that sharing is needed to prevent serious harm to you or someone else.

How AI is used here

I use AI assistants, including Claude (made by Anthropic), to help run the business side: sorting email, scheduling, bookkeeping and building this website. They work inside my own accounts, under my direction and my team's. I do not sell your information to AI companies, and I do not hand over what you share in a session to train anyone's AI.

How long I keep it

Applications and messages are kept while we're in conversation and for a reasonable time after. Client records, agreements and invoices are kept as long as tax and business law require. Ask me to delete something and I will, unless the law says I have to hold on to it.

Your choices

Write to to see what I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop hearing from me. Depending on where you live, the law may give you more rights than these. I honor those too.

Kids

This site and this work are for adults, 18 and over. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Other sites

This site links out to places I don't run, like Instagram, Substack, Spotify, Dr. Sam Rader's site and Holy Motherlover. Each one has its own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date at the top.

Questions

Write to any time. See also the Terms.
