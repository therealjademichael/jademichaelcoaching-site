What this site collects

This website itself does not collect personal information. Two outside services do the collecting:

  • The intake form on the Apply page is hosted by monday.com. What you type there (your name, phone, email, and what you're ready to shift) goes to monday.com and to Jade.
  • Booking for connection calls and coding sessions is handled by TidyCal, which has its own privacy policy.

This site saves one small setting in your browser's local storage: which preview color theme you picked. Nothing else.
