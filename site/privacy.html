Privacy

Your privacy, held with care.

What I collect, why, where it lives and who sees it. Plain words.

Last updated November 25, 2026.

Who runs this site

jademichael.co is run by Jade Michael Co LLC, a Utah company ("Jade," "I," "we"). Jade Michael Co LLC also owns Holy Motherlover LLC, which runs holymotherlover.com. The two share one contact list, so if you know me in both places, I know you once.

What this covers

This website, and what happens when you apply, book, become a client or join a list.

What I collect, and why

  • When you apply: your name, phone, email and what you're ready to shift. So I can answer you and show up prepared.
  • When you book: your name, email and the time you picked. So we both get a confirmation.
  • When we work together: your signed agreement, your session recordings, and your invoices and payments. So I can deliver the work and keep the books the law asks for.
  • When you join a list: your email. So I can send what you asked for. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • When you just visit: nothing personal. No ads, no ad trackers, no cookies of our own. The host keeps standard technical logs, like IP address and browser type.

I never sell your information. Not to anyone.

Where it's held

  • monday.com: applications and the contact list.
  • TidyCal: bookings.
  • Zoom: sessions and their recordings. Your recording is yours to keep.
  • PandaDoc: signed agreements.
  • QuickBooks (Intuit) and Stripe: invoices and payments. Card and bank details stay with them. I never see or store your full card number.
  • Google Workspace: email and files.
  • Netlify: hosts this site. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the music player on the Artistry page comes from Spotify.

Who helps me, and what they see

  • WvH Hospitality LLC runs my operations and bookkeeping. They see business records: names, emails, applications, invoices and payments. They do not sit in on sessions.
  • Co-producers. When I offer something together with another teacher or host, like a retreat, a course or a collaboration, the details you give to sign up are shared with that co-producer so we can run it together. The sign-up page will name them.
  • The law, if a court or a legal duty requires it, or if I believe in good faith that sharing is needed to prevent serious harm to you or someone else.

How AI is used

Some of the office work is done with AI assistants, including Claude (made by Anthropic). An assistant might read an application, a booking, an invoice or an email you sent, to organize it or draft a reply for a person to review.

  • No AI decides anything about you. A person does.
  • It never sees your card number. That stays with the payment company.
  • Your information is not used to train AI.
  • What you share in a session stays between us.

How long I keep it

Applications and messages: while we're in conversation and a reasonable time after. Client records, agreements and invoices: as long as tax and business law require.

Your choices

Write to to see what I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop hearing from me. I'll do it unless the law says I have to hold on to it. If the law where you live gives you more rights than these, I honor those too.

Kids

This site and this work are for adults, 18 and over.
