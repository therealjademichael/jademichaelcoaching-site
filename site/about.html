Where it started

Fifteen years inside a pattern.

I lived with OCD for fifteen years. I was so afraid that I washed my hands seventy times a day.

In 2019 I hit rock bottom and prayed: "God, show me the way, and I'll walk it."

So I walked it: Hermeticism, meditation, somatic work, subconscious reprogramming. I learned the thoughts were not me.

Years later, I stand here free.

Whatever you're walking through, it's not a curse. It's your initiation.

The work before this work

Neurosovereignty.

I created Neurosovereignty, a modality that blends cognitive psychology (the emphasis of my master's in experimental psychology), somatics, polyvagal theory and ancient alchemical principles.

It works through the mind, the body and the spirit, because we carry all three. Transform in all three and it goes so much deeper than working in just one.

"The only way out is through, and the only way through is in."

Then came Costa Rica

None of my tools touched it.

I had all these tools. Then in 2025, in Costa Rica, my nervous system crashed, and none of them were working. Life was handing me a deeper initiation. It was time to learn a deeper way of healing, and of being with reality.

So I reached out to Dr. Sam Rader, one of my best friends and favorite humans, a sister to me for years. She had created Source Code. She gave me one session. The next day I was in her facilitator training.

Now so much of the work I get to facilitate is Source Code, and I'm so honored to. My coaching is God- and Christ-centered, informed by Neurosovereignty. It doesn't have to be hard.
